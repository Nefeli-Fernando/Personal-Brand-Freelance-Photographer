# Daniel Osei Photography Portfolio

Daniel Osei Photography is a modern and responsive portfolio website created for freelance photographer Daniel Osei, specializing in wedding, portrait, and product photography for small businesses. The website is designed to showcase his photography style, highlight his best work, and provide potential clients with an easy way to explore services and get in touch.

The main purpose of this project is to create a professional online presence where clients can quickly discover Daniel's photography work, explore different categories, compare available packages, and send inquiries based on their specific requirements.

## Website Pages

* **Home Page**

  * Introduces Daniel Osei and his photography services
  * Displays featured work and creates a strong first impression
  * Provides visitors with a quick overview of his photography style

* **Wedding Photography Page**

  * Showcases Daniel's wedding photography projects
  * Highlights emotional moments, celebrations, and important memories captured during weddings
  * Allows clients to view wedding-specific work easily

* **Portrait Photography Page**

  * Features creative and professional portrait sessions
  * Displays different portrait styles and approaches
  * Helps clients explore portrait photography options

* **Product Photography Page**

  * Showcases product photography created for small brands and businesses
  * Demonstrates how products can be presented professionally through photography
  * Helps businesses understand the available services

* **Packages Page**

  * Provides details about available photography packages
  * Explains inclusions for different sessions such as half-day and full-day wedding shoots
  * Allows clients to compare options before contacting Daniel

* **Contact Us Page**

  * Provides a simple way for clients to send inquiries
  * Allows visitors to share their event details, preferred photography service, and requirements
  * Creates a direct communication channel with Daniel
